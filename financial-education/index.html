What is ADAMIS?

ADAMIS is a financial education program for primary schools in Spain. It teaches saving, budgeting, responsible consumption, debt, interest, investment, risk and financial safety.

Is ADAMIS only a workshop?

No. Schools can start with an initial workshop, but ADAMIS is built around full courses for 4th, 5th and 6th grade with assessment and progress tracking.

Which grades is ADAMIS designed for?

ADAMIS is designed for 4th, 5th and 6th grade primary students. Each course adapts financial education content to the maturity of the age group.

What makes the proposal innovative?

ADAMIS combines financial content, classroom activities, a learning platform, assessment and progress tracking to move beyond isolated talks into a complete learning journey.
