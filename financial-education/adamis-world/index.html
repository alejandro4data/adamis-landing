Character 02

Brote

Prudence, planning and consistency.

Brote breaks goals into steps and anticipates consequences. Brote brings order, but caution is not always the right answer.

Before deciding, Brote asks: “What will this mean later?”
  • Plan
  • Protect
  • Review

Character 03

Chispa

Initiative, curiosity and creativity.

Chispa spots opportunities, suggests alternatives and provides the push to get started. Chispa represents the desire to try, create and enjoy experiences.

That enthusiasm can lead to acting too quickly, but Chispa does not represent “bad spending”: saving does not mean giving up on enjoying life either.

  • Try
  • Create
  • Enjoy

All together

One team.
Three ways to learn.

Three personalities that complement one another.

Chispa brings the idea and Brote helps organise it; Brote spots a limit and Chispa finds an alternative.

They are not always in conflict. ADAMIS steps in when one of them moves towards an extreme and helps restore balance.

  • Imagine
  • Organise
  • Balance
