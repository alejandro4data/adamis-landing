REFERENCE FRAMEWORK

The content is not improvised either.

At ADAMIS, we develop our content using the financial competence framework for children and youth published jointly by the European Commission and the OECD/INFE as a reference.

  1. 01

    Money and transactions

  2. 02

    Planning and managing finances

  3. 03

    Risks and reward

  4. 04

    Financial landscape

We adapt this framework to Primary Years 4, 5 and 6 and turn it into content, decisions and experiences suited to each year group.
